    The fifteen licensed bureaus

    Independent forex bureaus on the National Bank of Ethiopia register, with the licence number each one trades under.

    Reading the board. Buying is what the bureau pays you for foreign cash; selling is what it charges you. Rates are per one unit of currency, in birr. Some bureaus quote a selling rate that excludes a service fee of up to 2 percent — confirm the all-in figure at the counter before you transact.
    Sources. Bureau rates are read from each bureau’s own published board where it has one, and entered by hand otherwise. The NBE indicative rate is the weighted average of the previous business day’s interbank transactions and is a reference, not a price anyone is obliged to offer.
